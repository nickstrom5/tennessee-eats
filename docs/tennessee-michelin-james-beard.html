What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide American South, which first covered Tennessee in November 2025 and revealed its 2026 selection in Nashville in October 2026, as MICHELIN lists it. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its winners, its 2026 nominee and semifinalists, and America's Classics awards from any year (2 places). Semifinalists are labeled as exactly that, and where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.
