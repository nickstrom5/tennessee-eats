Cayenne, white bread, pickles

Nashville hot chicken is fried chicken given a coat of cayenne, as an oily paste or a dry rub, and served on a slice of white bread with dill pickle chips on top. The Prince family's shack made it famous; Thornton Prince's version dates to the 1940s, and the James Beard Foundation named Prince's Hot Chicken Shack an America's Classic in 2013. Heat levels run from mild to dares, and most places ask before they fry.

No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Meat-and-threes often serve lunch only and barbecue joints close when the meat runs out, so check before you drive.
