Memphis barbecue

12 Memphis joints for the city's barbecue: dry-rub ribs, chopped pork shoulder on a bun with slaw, and barbecue spaghetti. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Dry rub, wet ribs, and spaghetti

Memphis barbecue is pork: ribs cooked dry with a spice rub or wet with sauce, and shoulder chopped or pulled for sandwiches topped with coleslaw. Barbecue spaghetti, noodles tossed in sauce with smoked pork, is a Memphis side you won't find many other places. The Rendezvous, in a basement off a downtown alley, has served charcoal-grilled dry-rub ribs since 1948.

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Meat-and-threes often serve lunch only and barbecue joints close when the meat runs out, so check before you drive.
